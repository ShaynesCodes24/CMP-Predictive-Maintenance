# CMP Technician Glossary

## CMP

Chemical Mechanical Planarization. A semiconductor process that uses chemical slurry and mechanical polishing to flatten wafer surfaces.

## Platen

Rotating table that holds the polishing pad.

## Carrier Head

Assembly that holds and applies pressure to the wafer during polishing.

## Slurry

Chemical abrasive fluid used during polishing.

## Pad Conditioner

Mechanism used to refresh the polishing pad surface and maintain process consistency.

## Retaining Ring

Consumable part around the wafer that helps control polishing uniformity.

## Preventive Maintenance

Scheduled maintenance intended to reduce unplanned downtime and tool faults.

## Predictive Maintenance

Maintenance approach that uses data trends to estimate when service may be needed before a failure occurs.
